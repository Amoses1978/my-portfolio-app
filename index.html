about

From bare-metal racks to

I'm a Cloud & DevOps Engineer who cares less about the tools in fashion and more about systems that stay up at 3am without a phone call. My work sits at the intersection of infrastructure, automation, and reliability — designing environments where deployment is boring, on purpose.

Over the past several years I've helped teams move from manual deploys and tribal knowledge to version-controlled, observable, self-service infrastructure. I write Terraform like application code, treat pipelines as products, and measure success in incidents that never happened.

Outside of production, you'll find me contributing to internal tooling, mentoring engineers on cloud fundamentals, and occasionally breaking my own homelab cluster on purpose to see how fast it recovers.
